    What I go deep on

    Content design and content engineering:

    • Designing how an agent talks: what it says, when it asks, when it hands over, and what it won’t do
    • Writing the system prompts and reference files that shape how a model behaves
    • Terminology and taxonomies that people and AI can both read
    • Regular quality checks on AI-written copy, so we know what’s getting better and what isn’t
    • AI workflows that connect Slack, Notion, Claude and n8n, to take the grunt work off everyone’s plate

    • Copy experiments and A/B tests, using behavioural principles like loss aversion and reward framing
    • Accessible copy, written to WCAG 2.1

    • Leading writers: hiring, onboarding and coaching, with a competency framework to grow against
